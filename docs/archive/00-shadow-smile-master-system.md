# PRIVACY PHILOSOPHY

ShadowSmile uses a:

> privacy-first trust model.

Privacy settings should always feel:
- understandable
- predictable
- emotionally safe

---

## Public Accounts

Public accounts allow others to:

- view posts
- view profile
- interact
- follow freely

Messaging permissions may still apply separately.

---

## Private Accounts

Private accounts restrict visibility to approved followers.

Only approved followers may:

- view posts
- interact
- view profile details
- view social connections

---

## Private Follow Logic

When a follow request occurs:

User may:
- accept
or
- deny

If accepted:

Approved followers may view content normally.

Founder clarification:

> private means hidden from outsiders.

NOT permanently hidden from approved followers.

---

## Messaging Separation Rule

Following permissions and messaging permissions are separate systems.

Following approval does NOT automatically grant unrestricted messaging access.

Privacy and safety take priority.
